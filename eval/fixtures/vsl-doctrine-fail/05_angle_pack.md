## Angles

### 1. The Night Watch Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Safety / Security
**Core Idea:** Night sounds don't just wake you — they steal the ability to know what's true.
**Headline:** *Night sounds never became an answer.*

**The Hollow:** You are the night watch. Ear to a tiny chest, counting rises that never become an answer. Every gurgle restarts the scan. You are not tired yet — you are uninformed, and that is worse.

**The Villain:** The hospital bulb that answers with a wet sound and hope. A sealed guess dispenser. It cannot show you whether the blockage left.

**The Dream:** Nights were normal again. She finally slept through; it was over.

**Story Arc:** I came home ready to be careful. Then the congestion started and I became a private check at 2am. Saline. Steam. The hospital bulb. Nothing proved he was getting air.

**Product Fit:** ClearCup's collection cup makes the mucus visible when it leaves, so night fear has an answer instead of another listen.

### 2. The Blind Shopping Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Agency / Control
**Core Idea:** Trying harder with tools that show you nothing is not a plan.
**Headline:** *I kept buying blind tools that showed nothing.*

**The Hollow:** Effort without feedback. You line up what you bought and still get nothing before the next feed. Control leaked out of empty results.

**The Villain:** Opaque weak clear-outs sold as upgrades: the sealed bulb, another tube, an electric that still doesn't show you the job.

**The Dream:** The unused extras stay in the drawer. You don't add a fourth box to the line.

**Story Arc:** Tried the cup → mucus visible → slept

**Product Fit:** ClearCup's visible collection is the feedback loop the category kept skipping.

**Awareness stage:** solution_aware
**Test priority:** 2

### 3. The Unshareable Help Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Relational Equity
**Core Idea:** When the tool needs someone's mouth, help stops being shareable.
**Headline:** *Help stopped being a job we could share.*

**The Hollow:** Help exists in the next room. He offered. Your body said no before your voice did. The night technician job became yours because the ritual required a mouth.

**The Villain:** The mouth-suction tube. It can move mucus and still decide who is allowed to parent the clear-out.

**The Dream:** You pass him the device without a speech. He does the clear-out. You stay a person in the doorway.

**Story Arc:** He offered. I said no. Steam and the bulb had already failed; the mouth tube worked and still isolated me. The twist wasn't that I was controlling — the tool had chosen the parent.

**Product Fit:** ClearCup clears into a visible cup without mouth contact, so the job can move between you.

**Hook Cluster:**
- The mould living in the sealed bulb
- I almost called 911 at 2am
- 20% off this week

**Awareness stage:** problem_aware
**Test priority:** 3
