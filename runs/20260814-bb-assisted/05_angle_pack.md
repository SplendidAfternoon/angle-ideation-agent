## Angles

### 1. The Dark Check Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Safety / Security  
**Core Idea:** Night sounds don't just wake you — they steal the ability to know what's true.  
**Headline:** *I checked his chest all night and still knew nothing.*

**The Hollow:** Every gurgle restarts the scan. You check the chest, the color, the silence between breaths — and still cannot tell if you are careful or collapsing.

**The Villain:** A blind bulb that answers with noise and hope, never with proof.

**The Dream:** You stand by the crib and realize you aren't counting. You're just there.

**Story Arc:** I came home ready. Then congestion made me a night watchman. Saline. Steam. The hospital bulb. Nothing told me what was happening. The twist wasn't that I was dramatic — I was working blind. Once mucus showed up in a clear cup, the math got quieter.

**Product Fit:** Baby Bubble's clear collection cup turns a blocked nose into something you can see leave — so the night stops being pure guesswork.

---

### 2. The Hidden Chamber Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Trust / Safety  
**Core Idea:** The tool you trusted to protect them may be the thing you should have feared.  
**Headline:** *The hospital bulb hid what I would never put in her nose.*

**The Hollow:** You thought nightly clearing was care. The idea of hidden growth in sealed rubber sits in your gut like betrayal with a receipt.

**The Villain:** A sealed rubber bulb you cannot see, clean, or dry inside — mould risk included.

**The Dream:** Open pieces on the counter. Clear cup. Nothing left to imagine growing in the dark.

**Story Arc:** The hospital sent us home with the blue bulb and a ritual. I obeyed. Then I thought about what a sealed chamber keeps. I didn't need another opaque promise. I needed parts I could see and wash.

**Product Fit:** Baby Bubble's clear, cleanable cup leaves nowhere for a hidden chamber to betray you.

### 3. The Solo Night Shift Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Agency / Partnership  
**Core Idea:** When the tool needs someone's mouth, "help" stops being shareable.  
**Headline:** *Mouth suction made me the only parent on nights.*

**The Hollow:** He offers. You refuse. Not because he's useless — because the ritual is too disgusting to hand over. You stay alone on purpose.

**The Villain:** Mouth-suction design that demands one parent's mouth and disgust tolerance.

**The Dream:** You pass him the device without a speech and without that knot.

**Story Arc:** He offered to take a night. I said no and hated how it sounded. The mouth tube decided who could parent the clear-out. The change wasn't a teamwork lecture. It was a tool without mouth contact.

**Product Fit:** Baby Bubble clears into a visible cup without mouth suction — handoff without the disgust barrier.

### 4. The Wrong Villain Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Guilt / Regret  
**Core Idea:** Feeling like a monster while helping means the ritual is wrong — not that you are.  
**Headline:** *Helping her breathe made me feel like the villain.*

**The Hollow:** Every cry during clearing lands as proof you are cruel. Protection and self-hatred arrive together.

**The Villain:** Blind, mean-feeling clear-out rituals that make care look like harm.

**The Dream:** A clear-out that feels like care — she settles, and you don't hate yourself for helping.

**Story Arc:** I wanted to protect her. Instead I felt like the villain. The twist: the guilt was aimed at me when it belonged to a method with no gentleness and no proof. Seeing the clear-out let me relocate the blame.

**Product Fit:** A clear cup makes help visible — Baby Bubble turns the ritual back into care.

### 5. The Blind Effort Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Agency / Control  
**Core Idea:** Trying harder with tools that show you nothing is not a plan.  
**Headline:** *I kept trying blind tools that showed me nothing.*

**The Hollow:** Effort without feedback. You press, release, hope — and still get "nothing comes out."

**The Villain:** Opaque weak clear-outs that reward sunk effort without evidence.

**The Dream:** Looking into the cup and seeing the job actually happened.

**Story Arc:** Bulb. Saline. Mouth tube. Each try felt like diligence. Lined up, they were the same blindness. I didn't need more force. I needed feedback.

**Product Fit:** Baby Bubble's clear collection cup gives you a result you can see — agency through proof.

### 6. The No Answer Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Authority  
**Core Idea:** You cannot be a capable parent in the dark if the tool never shows what happened.  
**Headline:** *Without seeing anything I stopped trusting my hands.*

**The Hollow:** Competence collapses into guessing. Ambiguity feels worse than a hard truth.

**The Villain:** The sealed guess dispenser — noise without an answer.

**The Dream:** Gunk in the cup. Done. Known.

**Story Arc:** I kept trying to fix it like a capable parent. The bulb gave noise. Not evidence. The twist: I needed sight. Once the clear-out was visible, judgment came back.

**Product Fit:** The clear cup is the answer layer — Baby Bubble restores knowing.

### 7. The Shift Workers Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Relational Equity  
**Core Idea:** When neither of you sleeps, you lose the partnership before you notice.  
**Headline:** *Congestion turned us into two tired shift workers.*

**The Hollow:** Conversation shrinks to handoffs. Tenderness becomes logistics. You are on the same team on paper and opponents by 3 a.m.

**The Villain:** Nightly failed clear-outs (bulb/mouth tools that never finish the job) that keep both adults awake.

**The Dream:** Same room. Shared quiet. A night that ends because the nose actually cleared.

**Story Arc:** We were a team. Congestion made us a rota. We blamed the shifts while the real fight was a blockage we couldn't see finished. Once the cup showed the clear-out, sleep — and "us" — had a path back.

**Product Fit:** Baby Bubble finishes the clear-out you can see — so the household can clock out together.

---

### 8. The Erased Self Angle

**Psychosocial Currency:** Identity / Selfhood  
**Core Idea:** Endless ON nights make a temporary crisis feel like your new personality.  
**Headline:** *Two a.m. clear-outs erased the person under mum.*

**The Hollow:** You project tonight's exhaustion onto forever. "Mum" is the only identity left — and even that feels like failing.

**The Villain:** Unfinished clear-out loops plus "just cope" advice that treats erasure as destiny.

**The Dream:** Catching your face in morning glass — ordinary recognition, not inventory.

**Story Arc:** I used to power down. Then 2 a.m. clear-outs made every hour a scan. I thought this was who I became. It wasn't a personality upgrade. It was an unfinished blockage. When the clear-out could end visibly, a version of me could return.

**Product Fit:** Baby Bubble shortens the always-ON night with a visible clear — the door back to yourself.
