## Angle Component Definitions

| Component | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Psychosocial Currency** | The underlying human emotional currency being spent (e.g. Vanity, Status, Relational Equity). |
| **Core Idea** | The single belief shift required to move the avatar from skepticism to openness. |
| **Headline** | A punchy, past-tense hook naming the specific loss or villain in avatar voice without naming the product. |
| **The Hollow** | A visceral, scene-level moment of grief, loss, or embarrassment that captures the problem state. |
| **The Villain** | The concrete tool, flawed advice pattern, or category failure that relocates blame away from the avatar. |
| **The Dream** | A concrete sensory moment of earned relief that captures what life feels like once resolved. |
| **Story Arc** | The 4-beat narrative progression: Setup -> Action -> Twist -> Verdict. |
| **Product Fit** | The causal bridge showing how the product mechanism solves the villain without making the brand the emotional climax. |
